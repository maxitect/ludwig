## Rules implemented

- **Enumeration** (`engine.ts`): geometric candidate origins per piece type (empty squares only, sliders stop at the first occupied square), every uncapture choice (`q r b n p`, mandatory for pawn diagonals), unpromote for non-pawn non-king pieces on the promotion rank, en passant and castle. Each candidate must pass `applyRetro`, then the prior must pass the material rules.
- **Material rules** on the prior position, per colour: no pawn on rank 1 or 8; at most 8 pawns; and pawns plus the promotion budget at most 8. The budget is the extra queens (above 1), rooks and knights (above 2), and bishops beyond one per square colour (this covers bishop-colour feasibility and plausible piece counts together).
- **Mode A:** exactly one survivor, equal to the authored ply.
- **Mode B (my reading of the ticket Note):** at step N the authored ply must be the only enumerated retro move after which the remaining authored plies still apply in order (`applyRetro` each). It must also equal the authored ply. The last step therefore needs a unique survivor outright.
- `toRetro` (DB-shaped `SolutionPly` to `Retro`) lives only in `engine.ts`. `LETTER_BY_PIECE` is now exported from `derive.ts` for it.

- New unique fixtures needed 8 pawns per side or an immobile army to rule out uncaptures; random search for such positions was slow, so `dev-unwind` was constructed by hand.
