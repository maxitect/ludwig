## Design

- `check.ts`: Mode A accepts exactly one ply. It runs `applyRetro` (which calls `replayMatches`) and compares `retroKey` with the authored ply. Mode B (`unwind`) throws "implemented in T030".
- `solver.tsx`: board shows the given position, with an arrow and highlights for the chosen retro move. The tray and an Unpromote toggle are always shown, so their presence does not hint at the answer. A drop is kept when some uncapture or unpromote choice makes it legal (the player then picks it); otherwise it is rejected inline with the engine's reason and the board is unchanged. Only a legal ply is written to attempt state and registered as the answer. Mode B puzzles show "not open yet".
- Registered the solver in `solvers.ts` and `attemptSchema` in `attempt-schemas.ts`. `load.ts` is unchanged (no S columns).

## Deviations and follow-ups

- **Content.** `dev-pawn-push` is now published (`publishedAt` 2026-10-01), because unpublished puzzles 404 on the solve route and AC1/AC2 need it. New `content/reverse-chess/dev-knight-takes.ts` is the uncapture puzzle, also published. `rnbqkb1r/pppppPpp/5pN1/8/8/8/PPPP1PPP/RNBQKB1R b - - 0 7`, solution e6 to f7 with a knight uncaptured. It is unique because the f7 pawn gives check, so only that pawn can have moved, and f6 and g6 are occupied, leaving e6. The 8-pawn black army makes the knight the only affordable uncapture. Both are dev titles; T032 should replace or unpublish them (the published dev titles appear in the catalogue).
