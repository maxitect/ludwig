## Design

- `check.ts`: Mode A accepts exactly one ply. It runs `applyRetro` (which calls `replayMatches`) and compares `retroKey` with the authored ply. Mode B (`unwind`) throws "implemented in T030".
- `solver.tsx`: board shows the given position, with an arrow and highlights for the chosen retro move. The tray and an Unpromote toggle are always shown, so their presence does not hint at the answer. A drop is kept when some uncapture or unpromote choice makes it legal (the player then picks it); otherwise it is rejected inline with the engine's reason and the board is unchanged. Only a legal ply is written to attempt state and registered as the answer. Mode B puzzles show "not open yet".
- Registered the solver in `solvers.ts` and `attemptSchema` in `attempt-schemas.ts`. `load.ts` is unchanged (no S columns).

## Deviations and follow-ups

- **Content.** Both dev fixtures stay unpublished (no `publishedAt`), so no "Dev:" title reaches the production catalogue. Unpublished puzzles 404 on the solve route, so local verification publishes them in `ludwig_t029` only (`update puzzles set published_at='2026-10-01' where slug in ('dev-pawn-push','dev-knight-takes')`). New `content/reverse-chess/dev-knight-takes.ts` is the uncapture puzzle: `rnbqkb1r/pppppPpp/5pN1/8/8/8/PPPP1PPP/RNBQKB1R b - - 0 10`, solution e6 to f7 with a knight uncaptured. It is unique because the f7 pawn gives check, so only that pawn can have moved, and f6 and g6 are occupied, leaving e6. The 8-pawn black army makes the knight the only affordable uncapture. Legality: a chess.js proof game reaching the exact FEN is in the file's docstring. T032 should replace both dev fixtures with real, published content.
- **Edit outside scope, tiny:** `uncapture-tray.tsx` (T028). The shadcn toggle rule `[&_svg:not([class*='size-'])]:size-4` shrank the piece glyphs to 16px; added `[&_svg]:size-full!` to the glyph wrapper and `p-0` to the item (the toggle's `px-3` otherwise squeezed the 36px wrapper to 20px).
- **Known, not fixed:** in Ink the black piece glyphs in the tray are nearly invisible (dark fill on dark button), the open T028 contrast question. The tray buttons keep their accessible names.
- **En passant and castling** retro moves cannot be entered in this UI (the engine supports them); `special` is always `none`. Needs a decision for T030/T032 (an en passant toggle, castle inferred from a king move of two files).
- The "Solved." stamp overlaps the "Solved in" text in `SolveChrome` at 1280px (screenshot `ac2-solved.png`); this is T018/T019 layout, not touched.
