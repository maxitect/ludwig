## Scope change: structured goals (migration ticket)

- Migrations `20261003203203_reverse_chess_goals` (generated) and `20261003203204_reverse_chess_goal_triggers` (custom). `reverse_chess_puzzles.goal_text` is dropped.
- `reverse_chess_goals` (puzzle_id pk, kind `retro_goal_kind`, display_text, unique (puzzle_id, kind)) plus subtype tables `_piece_on_square`, `_castling_right`, `_piece_count`, each FK (puzzle_id, kind) to the goal, with a generated `kind`.
- Triggers: deferred `trg_reverse_chess_goals_require_subtype`, and deferred `trg_reverse_chess_goal_matches_mode` (goal exists exactly when mode is `unwind`) on puzzles and goals. Tested in `tables.test.ts`.
- Payload carries only `goalText`; the predicate is read by `load-solution.ts` (`loadSolution` now returns `{ plies, goal }`). Payload leak test extended.
- `check` (Mode B): exactly N plies, each valid by `stepRetro` (applyRetro plus material rules), final prior satisfies the goal. Any such chain is accepted.
- `verify`: DFS over all chains of N enumerated retro moves, exactly one may reach the goal and it must be the authored one. Cost grows with N; not an issue at N=2, may need pruning for N around 5 to 6 (follow-up).
- `dev-unwind` now has a `piece_on_square` goal (black pawn on a7); still unique.

## Other changes

- En passant (toggle) and castling (inferred from a king dropped two files from e1/e8) can now be entered in both modes; `retro-ply.test.ts`. Not exercised in a browser (no fixture needs them).
- Solver split: `solver.tsx` dispatches to `last-move.tsx` and `unwind.tsx`; shared `retro-ply.ts`, `ply-choices.tsx`, `notation-toggle.tsx`.
- Notation: `SolverProps.chessNotation` (signed-in value, undefined when signed out) threaded through the solve page and `SolveChrome`; `saveChessNotation` action, `getUserChessNotation`/`setUserChessNotation`.
- `derive.ts` imports chess.js (SAN and legal moves for disambiguation); pure, but beyond "schema types only" in the folder contract.
- Out of scope edit: `retro-arrow.tsx` (T028) rounds path numbers to 4 decimals; sin/cos differed between Node and the browser and caused a hydration mismatch when a restored chain showed an arrow.
