## Deviations and follow-ups

- `Diagram` is `Pick<Payload, "slotCount" | "mIn" | "mOut" | "gears" | "meshes">`, derived from `schema.ts`. `occlusion` is not in it: the rule is unimplemented, as the ticket says.
- `spinSigns(gears, meshes, driverId)` returns `{ ok: true, signs }` or `{ ok: false, error: "not_bipartite" }`. Gears unreachable from the driver are coloured from `+1`.
- `stateAt`, `seeingCount` and `solveAll` throw on a non-bipartite graph or a missing driver.
- `sees` compares in integer units of 360/(teeth*slots) degrees, so the inclusive boundary is exact. Floats appear only in `facingDeg` and `bearingDeg`.
- No SPEC ambiguity found; the hand-computed fixtures matched first time. No SPEC edit.
