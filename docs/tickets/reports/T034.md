## Deviations and follow-ups

- `Diagram` is `Pick<Payload, "slotCount" | "mIn" | "mOut" | "gears" | "meshes">`, derived from `schema.ts`. `occlusion` is not in it: the rule is unimplemented, as the ticket says.
- `spinSigns(gears, meshes, driverId)` returns `{ ok: true, signs }` or `{ ok: false, error: "not_bipartite" | "disconnected" }`. A gear the driver can't reach gives `disconnected` (the user decided this during review).
- SPEC §5.2.2 (Mesh graph M) now says the graph must be connected, with every gear reachable from the driver, and that generators and `puzzles:verify` reject disconnected diagrams.
- `stateAt`, `seeingCount` and `solveAll` throw on a non-bipartite or disconnected graph, or a missing driver.
- `sees` compares in integer units of 360/(teeth*slots) degrees, so the inclusive boundary is exact. Floats appear only in `facingDeg` and `bearingDeg`.
- The hand-computed fixtures matched on the first run. Connectivity was ambiguous in the SPEC; the review settled it (see above).
