## Deviations and follow-ups

- User decision 1 (model change): gears progress one slot further each figure, `slot_g(f) = (s_g + (f-1)(S/2+1)) mod S`, S a multiple of 4. SPEC edits: §5.2.1 concept sentence, §5.2.2 S constraint and slot formula. `engine.ts` `geometry` updated, `schema.ts` refines `slotCount` (payload and content) to a multiple of 4, no migration (the DB check still only requires even). T034 fixtures recomputed (f=2..5 at crank 4; known win is now (4, 5, A)), and the F3 win list changed. New test shows a one-win diagram.
- User decision 2 (constructive generator): SPEC §5.2.3 rewritten as plant, cover with twin groups, local search, gate; Fix variant step adapted. Documented in `generate.ts`.
- User decision 3: only SPEC §5.2.1 (that sentence), §5.2.2 and §5.2.3 were edited.
- Findings that shaped the presets (measured, not assumed):
  - Mesh counts depend only on `u = c + mIn + (f-1)(mIn-mOut)`, so `mIn`/`mOut` never affect uniqueness; they only relabel the crank.
  - With all gears one tooth count, or with 8 or 16 slots, no unique diagram exists below 8 gears. With the realistic constraints (both spin signs, distinct slots) 6 gears at 45 degrees was never found (exhaustive and annealing searches); 7 works at 12 slots with teeth 8 and 16.
  - Hence easy uses exactly 7 gears (inside the ticket's 6 to 7), expert exactly 11 (inside 11 to 12). Even counts need a group of three twins, which does not fit at 12 slots with 8 and 16 teeth.
  - Teeth 12 and 24 gave unique diagrams but too few Fix variants with a unique repair (hard K=1 failed on 30 of 30 seeds), so every preset uses teeth 8 and 16. Difficulty comes from gear count and `h` (45 or 30). `presets.ts` is tunable after T037.
- Fix variants: a swap set means at most K pairwise-disjoint gear pairs (order independent). The stored set has exactly K pairs and is the only repair of size at most K. `repairsOf` uses the bit-parallel `masks.ts`, cross-checked against the engine in tests. `MAX_VARIANT_DIAGRAMS` is 5000 because K=1 variants are rare for some seeds.
- Extra file `src/puzzles/gears/masks.ts` (bit-parallel sees rule) and `src/puzzles/_shared/prng.ts` (`mulberry32`, `hashSeed`), the latter per the ticket; `engine.test.ts` now imports it. `scripts/make-grain.ts` untouched.
- Printed Fix diagrams have meshes between slots that are no longer adjacent; the ring-plus-chords rule holds for the repaired layout, which is the one players restore.
- `.verification/T035/` scratch scripts were moved out of the repo because lint covers them.
