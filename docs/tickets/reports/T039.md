## Deviations and follow-ups

- AC1 and AC2 wording is stale against the slot model (SPEC §5.2.2); results follow the engine.
- `motion` was already in `package.json` and installed (used by the solved stamp), so nothing was added.
- `engine.ts`: `signsOf` is now exported and the slot formula is extracted as exported `slotOf`, used by both `geometry` and `dance.ts`. No behaviour change.
- The convergence `<select>` is removed. The visible readout is now the `aria-live` line, empty while the dance moves. `data-testid="seeing-count"` stays on the count.
- Keys step one half-phase with no animation; Play and drag are the only interpolated paths. Releasing a drag snaps to the nearest half-phase.
- Pausing mid-phase leaves the persisted convergence at the last one landed on.
- SPEC §5.2.4 updated with the scrubber semantics.
- The dance uses a straight line for the out leg, so a gear passes close to the victim marker mid-leg.
