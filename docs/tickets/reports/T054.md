## Notes

- Glyphs: 26 SVGs generated by `scripts/dev/generate-glyphs.ts` from `content/lookups.ts` (five head shapes by five poses, plus the eyeball-headed figure for E). They are drawn through a CSS mask, so they take the ink token colour in both themes. `asset_key` is `glyph-NN` (checked by the DB), and the numbering is shuffled against the alphabet.
- `pictogram_glyphs` lives in `src/puzzles/pictogram-cipher/tables.ts` with the other tables; `scripts/seed-pictogram-glyphs.ts` upserts it by id (separate from `seedLookups` so the existing seed tests' custom lookups are unaffected), covered by `scripts/seed-pictogram-glyphs.test.ts`. Glyphs are never removed; deleting a used glyph fails on a named FK (`tables.test.ts`).
- No plaintext column: the plaintext is derived from symbols and glyph letters. Payload: `words` (asset keys) and `given` (asset key and letter). Attempt state is `{ guesses: [{ assetKey, letter }] }` and never includes given glyphs.
- Shared panel generalised: `CipherKeyPanel` takes optional `symbols` (render and accessible name per cipher letter) and `locked` (given guesses, read-only but focusable). Caesar and keyword unchanged (their unit and e2e tests pass). The solver maps each glyph to a puzzle-local cipher letter in numeric key order, so the panel's logic is reused.
- Uniqueness: without a dictionary, "inferable" is defined structurally in `verify.ts` (a word of 3+ letters with at most two unknown glyphs and at least half its letters known gives those glyphs away, repeated to a fixpoint), plus at least 20 letters, 8 distinct glyphs, given glyphs used, and at least one glyph left to solve. The mapping is global, so the plaintext fixes every glyph. SPEC 7.4.4 pictogram row updated.
- `e2e/helpers/db.ts` gets `pictogramAttemptFor` (the table has no `answer` column, so it does not fit `cipherAttemptFor`'s union).
- Content: 5 puzzles in `content/pictogram-cipher/`; titles and slugs do not echo the messages.
