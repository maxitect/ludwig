## Deviations and follow-ups

- Migration `20261009223545_cctv_maze_tables` adds the `wall_side` enum and the five tables; `compass8` is imported from `src/puzzles/sightlines/tables.ts`. No trigger migration: steps name no content row, so no redundant column and no FK to non-cascade.
- Camera cones ignore maze walls (they are laid over the street plan), so `deriveSeen` passes `isBlocked: () => false` to `visibility.ts`. SPEC section 7.4.4 cctv-maze row rewritten to describe this and the rest of the type.
- Content is `rows`, `cols`, `startRow/Col`, `exitRow/Col`, a flat `walls` list and `cameras`. The 5 mazes (`punt-lane`, `market-hill`, `kings-parade`, `backs-path`, `labyrinthos`, difficulty 1 to 5, 6x6 up to 12x12) were found by a seeded search (a perfect maze plus extra openings plus random cameras), accepted only if `puzzles:verify` passes, the cameras force a longer route than the camera-free one, and they cover at least a sixth of the board. All published 2026-10-09, so the type leaves "Coming soon".
- The reveal hint is a new action `revealCameras` (`src/lib/actions/cctv-maze.ts`, data in `src/lib/data/cctv-maze.ts`). It writes one `attempt_hints` row of kind `reveal_all` per attempt (re-toggling adds none) and nothing when signed out. The payload carries `puzzleId` for it, as spot-difference does. The reveal state is not saved: after a reload the cones are hidden again.
- The solver never blocks entering a cell a camera sees (that would reveal the cones); the player learns it from Check. Walls are visible, so they are blocked client-side. A path may revisit cells; stepping back onto the previous cell pops it.
- `attempt-schemas.ts` has the client-safe `cctv-maze` attempt schema; the solver is registered in `solvers.ts`.
- Out of scope per ticket and untouched: moving cameras and time steps.
