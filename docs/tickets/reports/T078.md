### Timing and batching
366 days take 1.6s against local Postgres. On Neon the cost is dominated by round trips; the route sets `maxDuration = 300`, and with the daily steady state (one new day per run) the work is a single insert. A first fill that did not fit would resume on the next run, since existing dates are skipped, so no explicit batching was added.
