## Design notes

- Nothing is stored for the track. `load.ts` builds the payload from the counts, the ends and the given pieces; `loadSolution` runs `engine.solve`. `check` accepts any layout that obeys the rules, so it never compares with the solution.
- `engine.ts`: `trace` walks from the entry and reports `path`, `reachesExit`, `breakAt` (the last cell of a track that stops short), `strays` and `branches`. `countSolutions` and `solve` share one DFS over simple paths from the entry, pruned on row and column counts, the given pieces and the free cells each line still needs. A 8x8 puzzle is verified in milliseconds.
- `check` names cells for: a given piece that was changed or left out, strays (a separate loop included), the cell where the path stops short, path cells a stray piece opens onto, and pieces in a line over its count.
- The solver is its own grid (counts need headers), like the nonogram's. A drag lays pieces inferred by `engine.laidAlong` (corners, and turning toward the light at the entry or exit); backing up a drag undoes the cell; a tap or Enter cycles empty, the six pieces, a cross and empty; keys 1 to 6 lay a piece, `x` crosses, Backspace clears. Given pieces are shaded and fixed. A count turns red, is underlined and gets a `!` when over, and fades when complete. The answer is registered, and a check requested, once every count is met.
- Content: five puzzles (night-train 6x6, branch-line 6x6, last-stop 7x7, signal-box 7x8, terminus 8x8), each with two given pieces. They were generated backwards from a random self-avoiding track (counts derived from it, then given pieces added until the search finds one track), so none copies a published grid.

## Deviations and follow-ups

- AC2 says "the same puzzle with a count removed (2+)". A count cannot be absent (the column is NOT NULL, SPEC §7.4.4), so the 2+ fixture is the same grid with no given piece, which is the loosened clue set.
- SPEC §2.4 calls the exit the "red light" and the entry the "green light". The palette has no green and red is the only accent, so the entry is an ink disc and the exit a red square. SPEC §2.4 now says so.
- SPEC §7.4.4 (railroad row): added that the ends are CHECKed to lie in the grid, and how `loadSolution`, `puzzles:verify` and `check` treat the track.
- `src/config/raw-tokens.test.ts` allowlists `bg-paper` for `src/puzzles/railroad/solver.tsx` (cells stay paper in Ink, as for the other grids).
- A drag that starts in the middle of existing track lays the start cell straight (its other side is unknown). It is correct at a straight piece; at a corner the player taps or presses a key to fix it. The e2e splits its two drags at a straight piece for this reason.
- The sort order of `railroad` in the spatial category is 4 (after spot-difference).
