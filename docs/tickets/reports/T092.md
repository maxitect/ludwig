## Scene proposal

Scene key and content slug: `cambridge-night` (`src/puzzles/detective-scene/scenes/cambridge-night.tsx`, `content/detective-scene/cambridge-night.ts`).

Layout concept (1200 by 800 units): a night street seen from the pavement, ink line in `currentColor`, with hatching and stipple only. The sky takes the top 15%: a thin moon, chimney pots and a skyline of pinnacles. The left third is a college wall with a gatehouse, oriel window, drainpipes, a noticeboard and bike racks at its foot. The centre is a small stone bridge over the Cam, with a punt moored below, a lamp-lit pub on the near bank and a bus stop in front. Across the right two thirds of the frontage runs a terrace of five shopfronts (butcher, newsagent, chemist, bakery, solicitors) with flats above and awnings. The foreground pavement holds a phone box, post box, bins, parked cars with number plates, stacked crates and a lamp post covered in notices. Titles are spread so no two regions overlap, mixing large hides (shopfront signs) with small ones (a lone number plate, a newspaper under a bin). Target SVG size under 40 KB, to be measured at the solve page.
