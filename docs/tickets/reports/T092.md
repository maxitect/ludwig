## What was built

- `src/puzzles/detective-scene/scenes/cambridge-night.tsx`: 1200 by 800 night street in ink line, redrawn in review. Night is hatching and light is the clean card fill, so it reads as night in both themes. A chapel behind eight different frontages (college gatehouse, pub, bookshop with an optician above, theatre, barber, dry cleaner, bakery, fishmonger), then a pavement of street life and a road with a car, bus, taxi and van.
- Titles sit on objects: notices on the college board, a staircase name board, an optician's eye chart ("S / HE / RLO / CK"), a wine crate, a beer barrel, a receipt in a bin, a mime poster, a dry-cleaning ticket, a jumper on a washing line, an iced cake, a lost-cat flyer, a street nameplate, graffiti ("LUTHER WOZ ERE"). Four letter styles are used, all token fonts. More than a dozen pieces of lettering are decoys, such as FREE HOUSE, OPTICIAN and TRUMPINGTON, so not every sign is a title.
- Nothing is traced. The Radio Times spread is a photo collage with oblique references; no drawing, position or gag is shared with it.
- `content/detective-scene/cambridge-night.ts`: 46 items. Regions are hand-placed over each object, at least 30 by 30 units, so every one contains a keyboard sector centre (`verify.test.ts`).
- `verify.ts`: minimum of 30 titles (`MIN_SCENE_TITLES`), checked after the per-item checks.
- All lettering is upper case, so the solve-page leak test still holds (titles are in mixed case).

## Deviations and follow-ups

- **`fixture-street` removed.** Neon `main` (br-green-field-zabr2lmo) holds `detective-scene/fixture-street` with 0 attempts, so the seed removes it without confirmation and the production build is not blocked. The scene component and content file are deleted. Unit and DB tests use a test-only `src/puzzles/detective-scene/test-content.ts` (three titles, scene key `test-street`, never seeded) for rejection cases, and the real `cambridge-night` content for load, payload and check tests. The e2e spec runs on `cambridge-night`.
- **Redundant aliases dropped.** `verify` rejected aliases that normalise to their own title ("Cagney & Lacey", "Law & Order", "Scott & Bailey", "Dalziel & Pascoe", "Z Cars", "Softly Softly", and "The"-less forms such as "Bill", "Sweeney", "Wire", "Professionals"), because matching already ignores case, punctuation, a leading "The" and "&"/"and". They are accepted anyway.
- **Hiding ideas reworked in review.** The approved titles are unchanged, but the sign-grid layout was replaced (see Review). On a phone the scene is read by zooming, which the solver supports; every title is readable at maximum zoom.
- **Seed risk to re-check.** A first attempt on `fixture-street` in production before this merges would make the non-interactive seed fail with "removal not confirmed". It had 0 attempts at review. `SEED_CONFIRM_REMOVE=detective-scene/fixture-street` clears it.
- **Keyboard e2e** no longer solves the whole puzzle (46 titles by sector cursor is slow); it finds three, reloads, and checks persistence. The full solve is the new pointer test.
- Preview snapshot updated: the generic preview draws five rings (the cap), previously three.
- SPEC §2.4: the minimum of titles per scene is now 30.
- E2E needs `VERCEL_ENV=preview` and `BETTER_AUTH_URL` set to the server's origin when run against `next start` (as in CI), or sign-up never completes.
