### Design notes

- The picture lives in `nonogram_cells` **(S)** and the caption in `nonogram_puzzles.caption` **(S)**. `load.ts` derives the clues from the cells through `loadSolution`, so only the size and clues reach the payload. `check` accepts any picture whose runs match the clues (the puzzle has exactly one) and returns the caption as `epilogue` only when correct.
- `engine.ts` has a line solver (prefix and suffix feasibility over run placements) and `countSolutions` (line propagation, then branching on the first undecided cell, capped at 2). Verification of the largest grid takes about 11 ms.
- Content is written as `#` and `.` rows with a caption. Titles and slugs name nothing about the picture.
- The solver has its own grid (clues need their own headers), not `RegionMarkGrid`: tap or Enter cycles fill, cross and empty, a drag along a row or column paints a run, Space fills, `x` crosses, Backspace clears. Each cell's accessible name carries its row and column clue, and a satisfied clue greys out.
