## Engine API (for T107)

All pure, in `src/puzzles/reflections/engine.ts`. `Layout` is `{ rows, cols, fixedMirrors, slots, ports }` (the `Content` shape).

- `trace(layout, mirrors, port)` returns `{ path: Step[], exit: PortRef }`. `mirrors` are the placed slot mirrors (`{ row, col, mirror }[]`); a fixed mirror wins a clash, a slot with no mirror is open floor. `Step` is `{ row, col, heading }`, the heading on entry, and a cell crossed twice appears twice. A beam from an edge cannot loop, so `exit` is always set (it can be on the entry side but never the entry port).
- `solutions(layout, limit = Infinity)` returns every `Placed[]` (one mirror per slot, reading order) joining every lettered pair; `countSolutions(layout, cap = 2)`; `solve(layout)` is the first or null. Search is DFS over slots in reading order and prunes by following one beam per pair until it leaves or hits an unassigned slot.
- Also exported: `pairsOf(layout)`, `unmatchedPorts(layout, mirrors)`, `entryOf`, `portOnEdge`, `portKey`, `SIDES`, `STEP`, `OPPOSITE`.
- A generator needs: random mirrors, choose slots, trace `trace(..., slots-true, port)` from edge ports, label pairs whose exit is a different port, then shrink labels while `countSolutions === 1`. The throwaway script that made the 5 files did exactly this (about 0.7 s for 8x8 with 16 slots); it is not committed.

## Notes

- Content: 5 original puzzles, `shop-window` 5x5 (8 slots, 4 labels), `glass-alley` 6x6 (10 slots, 4 labels), `double-take` 6 rows by 7 columns (12 slots, 5 labels), `hall-of-mirrors` 7x7 (14 slots, 6 labels), `last-light` 8x8 (16 slots, 6 labels). Every slot is forced: `check.test.ts` flips each slot of `shop-window` and the answer is rejected.
- Decisions taken under the "recommended option" rule: unlettered ports are not stored (the SPEC table has `label NOT NULL`), so they are neither drawn nor constrained; mirrors/ports/cells have `0..9` CHECKs and `contentSchema` mirrors the table limits (4 to 10, uppercase letter, side `n/e/s/w`) plus a cell-in-grid refinement; port edge placement, duplicates and label counts are checked in `verify`, as the ticket asks.
- `check` is rule-based like fillomino: it returns `{ correct, wrongParts }` where `wrongParts` are lettered ports `{ side, offset }` whose beam leaves by a port other than its partner, traced from the player's answer only. `correct` also needs exactly one mirror in each slot and none elsewhere. `loadSolution` solves the clues (nothing is stored). `checkCell` and `revealCell` are not exported (a beam puzzle has no per-cell value to reveal).
- Solver is its own board (not `CellGrid`), because ports sit outside the grid: a `role="grid"` of cells inside a CSS grid ring of camera buttons, with one SVG overlay for beams in cell units. Satisfied pairs draw in ink and their cameras fill ink; the focused, hovered or tapped camera draws a red pencil beam with a `beam-draw` keyframe (new in `globals.css`, applied with `motion-safe:` so both the OS preference and the account's reduce-motion setting turn it off). A live line under the board states where the shown beam exits. Tapping a camera pins its beam for touch devices.
- The beam is redrawn on every mirror change because the polyline is re-keyed on the placed mirrors.
- Migration was renamed from drizzle-kit's random name to `20261102234511_reflections_tables` before it ran anywhere shared.
- `content/lookups.ts`: `reflections` is in the spatial category with sort 6. `src/config/raw-tokens.test.ts` allowlists `bg-paper` for the solver, as for railroad. SPEC §7.4.4 reflections row updated for `loadSolution`, `check` and the verify rules.
