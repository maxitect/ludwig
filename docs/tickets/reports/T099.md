## Findings that change things

- **The blog is light on method.** KrazyDad names a Dancing Links brute-force solver as the base of his sudoku generator, "software that generates" kakuro, and Tatham's code tweaked for slitherlink. It gives no removal strategy, grader, clue counts or symmetry policy. It does state the bar: books solvable "without resorting to trial and error", and an Insane tier that allows it. Recommendations on strategy, mapping and symmetry rest on general practice, and the document says so where that applies.
- **Hand difficulties look unreliable.** A prototype singles-only solver solves the hand-written "difficulty 5" sudoku (`dead-end`) and leaves 25 cells open on the "difficulty 2" one (`back-room`). The prototype may be wrong, so T100 must run a real grader over hand-written content and update `meta.difficulty` where it differs by two or more.
- **Generation is cheap.** Minimal sudoku by random removal: median 2.1 ms (1.3 ms symmetric), 22 to 27 clues, 25 to 30 with 180° symmetry. Futoshiki 5×5 by sign removal: median 2.5 ms. Singles alone solve 44% of unsymmetric and 68% of symmetric random minimal puzzles, so tiers 1 and 2 will fill easily and tier 5 will be thin.
- **Recommendation:** generated grid puzzles meet unique plus grader-solvable; hand-written ones need only unique. Generated content is committed with a typed `generated` provenance export (generator, version, seed; difficulty from `meta.difficulty`), not seeded, and `puzzles:verify` regenerates and compares. Only dailies are materialised, via a generic daily table (T111).

## Deviations and follow-ups

- Not recommended for generation: nonogram, chess problem, crossword, logic grid, knights and knaves, napkin maths, rota, gear train, sightlines, CCTV maze, word types, ciphers, odd one out, acrostic, detective scene. Reasons are per row in section 3.
- Pages were first read through a summarising fetch tool; at review every cited page was re-read as plain text (see Review).
- Tatham's puzzle collection is open source, but its licence was not checked and no code is proposed for reuse.
- The cipher hooks check structure only (at least 20 letters, one key), which is fine, and nothing in this research changes that.
