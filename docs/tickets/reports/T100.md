## Design

- `src/puzzles/_shared/generate/pipeline.ts`: `seedRng` (`mulberry32(hashSeed("<seed>:<difficulty>"))`), `shuffle`, `generateGraded` (candidate loop), `resolveVersion`. `candidates.ts`: pencil-mark state, shared techniques (naked and hidden singles, naked and hidden subsets) and the easiest-first ladder driver.
- `sudoku/grade.ts`, `futoshiki/grade.ts`; `sudoku/generate.ts`, `futoshiki/generate.ts` (frozen `{1: generateV1}` maps).
- `src/puzzles/generators.ts`: registry keyed by generator name, `generatedSchema`, `regenerate`, `gradeContent`. `scripts/content-files.ts` reads an optional `generated` export; `puzzles:verify` regenerates before the hook (so an edited file reports a regeneration mismatch); `db:seed` ignores it.
- CLIs (`scripts/dev/`): `puzzles:gen`, `puzzles:grade [--write]`, `time-generators.ts`. The content-file formatter is `scripts/format-content.ts`, outside `scripts/dev/` because the CI regeneration test imports it.

Ladder (difficulty = hardest technique level):
- Sudoku: 1 naked single; 2 hidden single; 3 pointing, box/line, naked pair, hidden pair; 4 x-wing, xy-wing; 5 swordfish, xyz-wing, naked triple, hidden triple.
- Futoshiki: 1 naked and hidden singles plus signs against a placed digit; 2 sign bounds (min/max, which also covers chains); 3 naked pair; 4 hidden pair, naked triple; 5 hidden triple. Size 5 for difficulty 1-2, 6 for 3-4, 7 for 5.
- Soundness checked over 300 random minimal puzzles (no technique ever removed the true digit).

## Deviations and follow-ups

- **For the user: three hand-written puzzles are flagged** (sudoku back-room, futoshiki tall-order and fine-margins). The grader could not finish them, so they stay published at 5. They may need trial and error, or a technique the grader lacks (the futoshiki ladder has no sign-aware advanced techniques). Keep, rewrite or replace.
- Meta of existing files was kept; only `difficulty` changed. `content/weekly.ts` references no sudoku or futoshiki, so no week is affected.
- **Research conflict, regrade threshold.** Research sections 5.2 and 7 say to update a hand-written difficulty only where it differs from the grade by two or more; the ticket and AC6 say wherever it differs. AC6 was followed, so sudoku cold-case moved 3 to 2 (a difference of one). Under the research rule it would have stayed at 3.
- **Research deviation, sudoku ladder.** Research section 5.3 puts swordfish and XYZ-wing at 4 and chains (colouring, XY-chain, ALS) at 5. The grader has no chains, so 5 is swordfish, XYZ-wing, naked triple and hidden triple, and 4 is X-wing and XY-wing. Research marks the cut points as a proposal to tune.
- The ladder cut points differ from research section 5.3 for futoshiki (chains of signs are covered by iterated bounds propagation, so "chains" is not a separate rung); sudoku level 1 is naked singles only per the research, so "singles" in AC3 is read as naked singles.
- Futoshiki tier 5 needs 7 by 7 grids to be reachable; 6 by 6 never produced one in 10 seeds.
- The futoshiki property test samples tier 5 on every twentieth seed to keep `pnpm test` fast (tier 5 takes seconds per puzzle).
- The CLI takes the generator name as `<type>`; a variant generator whose name differs from its type key (T108) will need a type argument.
- Generated puzzles use `publishedAt` of the generation day; SPEC unchanged (T099 text matched the implementation).
