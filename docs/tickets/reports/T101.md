## Design

- `troix/generate.ts`: generator `troix`, version 1, registered in `src/puzzles/generators.ts` (generator and grader). Per candidate: a size (d1 6x6, d2 6x6 or 9x9, d3 to d5 9x9), a random filled grid by seeded backtracking, then givens removed in shuffled order while the grader, held to the target level, still solves it. Kept when the grade equals the target and `countSolutions === 1`.
- `troix/grade.ts` (`solveTroix`, `gradeTroix`): per-cell symbol domains. Ladder: line at quota and pair ends (1), gap rule (2), only places: a symbol a line needs with exactly that many cells able to hold it (3), line arrangements: enumerate every valid arrangement of one line and drop symbols none uses (4).
- Difficulty 5 is the research tie-break on hard steps (`docs/research/generators.md` §5.3): line arrangements needed 3 or more times. The ticket names the given count instead, but it barely moves (27 to 36 givens at 9x9 for level-4 puzzles, median 31), while the number of line-arrangement steps spreads (over 126 level-4 puzzles: 1 step 55, 2 steps 49, 3 steps 19, 4 steps 3). The research wins on conflict. No technique assumes a symbol and tests it, so no puzzle needs trial and error (SPEC §4 authoring rule, research §5.2).
- SPEC "Generated content" lists troix.
