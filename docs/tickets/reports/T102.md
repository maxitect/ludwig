## Design

- `star-battle/generate.ts`: generator `star-battle`, version 1, registered in `src/puzzles/generators.ts` (generator and grader). Per candidate: a size and K from the difficulty (d1 5-6, d2 6-7, d3 7-8 or K=2 at 8, d4 8-9 or K=2 at 8, d5 K=1 at 9-10), a random valid star placement, regions grown around it (K=1: from the stars; K=2: each pair of stars joined by a shortest path first), then repair. Repair takes the rival solutions the engine finds and moves a boundary cell between regions (preferring rival stars, never a planted star, keeping regions connected and at least K+2 cells), so the planted solution stays valid, until `countSolutions === 1`. Rejected: a region under K+2 cells and a region that is one straight line over 3 cells. Kept when the grader solves it at exactly the target.
- `star-battle/grade.ts`: `solveStarBattle` (grade and stars) and `gradeStarBattle`. Ladder, easiest first: saturated unit (level 1, also fills a unit with as many open cells as stars missing, and clears neighbours of a star); single region against lines (1); single line against regions (2); 2x2 blocks (3: a cell is empty if a star there leaves some unit fewer aligned 2x2 blocks than stars it needs, a star if an empty cell does); 2 lines/regions (4); 3 and 4 lines/regions (5). The counting technique takes n lines of one direction and the regions they touch (or the reverse), and when both need the same number of stars the touching side's cells outside the chosen side are empty.
- K=2 puzzles at 9 and 10 rarely solve by these techniques (about 70% need a guess), so d5 uses K=1 only; K=2 appears at d3 and d4.
- `scripts/dev/time-generators.ts` takes optional generator names after N so one generator can be timed.
- SPEC "Generated content" lists star battle.
