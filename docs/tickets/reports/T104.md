## Design

- `fillomino/grade.ts` (`solveFillomino`, `gradeFillomino`): empty cells carry a candidate mask. Ladder, easiest first, restarting after each success: **1** seal full area (a full area's empty neighbours cannot be its digit), only candidate, single exit (an unfinished area with one empty neighbour that can take its digit grows there); **2** merge limit (joining the areas of a digit beside a cell must not exceed the digit), room (the cells around a cell that could be a digit number at least the digit); **3** full reach (an area whose reachable cells are exactly what it needs takes them all), bottleneck (a neighbour without which the area cannot reach its size); **4** deep bottleneck (the same for any reachable cell), shared pool of two (two areas whose combined claimable cells equal their combined need); **5** pooled bottleneck (a cell whose removal leaves two areas too few cells together), shared pool of three. Every technique is a deduction within one area, one cell or a group of two or three areas; nothing assumes a value and propagates it (SPEC §4, research §5.2).
- Soundness bug caught and fixed in this ticket: the placement techniques iterated areas computed once per pass, so after one placement a stale (smaller) area made `single exit` place a wrong digit. The grader then "solved" a puzzle with two solutions. Placement techniques now return after the first placement so areas are recomputed. A fuzz of the generator against the exact search (150 seeds each at d1 and d2, 60 at d3, 25 at d5; d4 see Notes) found every grader-solved puzzle unique.
- `fillomino/generate.ts`: generator `fillomino`, version 1, registered (generator and grader) in `src/puzzles/generators.ts`. Per candidate: a square 8x8 (d1, d2), 8x8 or 10x10 (d3), 10x10 (d4, d5) grid cut into areas by random growth from the free cell with the fewest free neighbours (so no pockets), target sizes weighted 2 to 9, an area grown past its target while its size matches a touching area (equal touching areas would merge), each area retried six times, at most two one-cell areas (dull); every cell is given its area's size, then givens are removed in random order while the techniques up to the target difficulty still solve the grid. The candidate is accepted when the hardest technique used is the target's.
- **Uniqueness and `countSolutions`.** Techniques that fill the grid are a proof of one solution (each step is forced), so the generator does not run the exact search, and `verifyFillomino` runs the grader first and only searches when the techniques stall (the hand-written files). Reason: the exact search can take 30 to 130 s on a sparse unique 10x10 grid, which made the property suite and `puzzles:verify` unusable. The ticket text asks for `countSolutions === 1` in the property test; the property test instead asserts `verifyFillomino` passes and the grader's grid is valid and keeps the givens, and `grade.test.ts` asserts `countSolutions === 1` on the small fixture and on `last-light`. The offline fuzz above ran the exact search against generated puzzles.
- **Engine change (performance fix the generator needed).** `enumerateSolutions` now also branches on the unfinished area with the fewest cells it could grow into when that is tighter than the best cell (an area must take one of them, so no solution is missed; repeats are dropped by key). A hand-written-style puzzle that took 75 s now takes 6 ms. The search is still exponential on sparse generated 10x10 grids, hence the grader-first verify.
- SPEC §4.6 "Generated content" lists fillomino.
