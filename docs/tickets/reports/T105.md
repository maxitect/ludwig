## Design

- `norinori/grade.ts` (`solveLayout`, `solveNorinori`, `gradeNorinori`): cells are open, shaded or empty. Ladder, easiest first: **1** region of two (a region with exactly the open cells it still needs shades them; a full region empties the rest), domino completion (a cell in a finished domino empties its other neighbours; a lone shaded cell with one possible partner shades it) and no touching (an open cell that could be in no domino is empty); **2** shared neighbour (an open cell beside every possible partner of a lone shaded cell is empty) and small region ways (region ways on a region of at most three open cells); **3** partner ways (a possible partner that breaks a rule on the spot is empty) and region ways (every way a region could take the cells it needs, checked on the spot; a cell in none is empty, in all is shaded); **4** neighbouring regions (a way is also dropped when it leaves a neighbouring region no way of its own); **5** region overlap (two neighbouring regions' ways taken together). "On the spot" is the rule check after the move and the emptying the no-touching rule implies around a finished domino, with no propagation of techniques: nothing assumes a value and propagates it (SPEC §4 authoring rule, research §5.2, T101 and T106 reviews).
- `norinori/generate.ts`: generator `norinori`, version 1, registered (generator and grader) in `src/puzzles/generators.ts`. Per candidate: a square size from the difficulty (d1 5 to 6, d2 6 to 7, d3 7 to 8, d4 8 to 9, d5 9 to 10); the densest of four maximal random packings of non-touching dominoes (maximal, so no two cells side by side are out of every domino's reach); regions laid in a **cascade** the level-1 techniques solve in order: an order over the dominoes (those with the most cells only they can clear first), a share of them bare two-cell regions (d1 33% to d5 5%), and every other cell joining the smallest neighbouring region later in the order than the domino that clears it (a cell beside no domino waits for all its neighbours); then **hardening**: random boundary moves (a cell into a neighbouring region, or two planted cells of neighbouring regions swapped so their dominoes cross the border) kept while the techniques up to the target still solve the grid, until the hardest technique needed is the target's. Rejected afterwards: a region over 8 cells (d1 to d2) or 10 cells (d3 to d5), a straight line of more than four cells, and `countSolutions !== 1` (never seen: a solve by sound techniques is a proof of uniqueness).
- **Ticket conflict, research wins.** The ticket's "repair region boundaries until `countSolutions === 1`" (the WIP's hill-climb on the exact solver, which hit the 4000-candidate cap at d1 and needed trial and error for most unique layouts) is replaced by construction and repair gated on the technique grader, which research §5.2 names as the bar; `countSolutions` is still asserted on every candidate and in `verify`.
- The AC2 cycle-1 failure: the planted-cell swap checked each region's connectivity before the swap; when the two swapped cells were each other's only link to their new regions, a region came apart. Moves now check both regions after the change. Content was regenerated.
- SPEC §4.6 "Generated content" lists norinori.
