## Design

- `src/puzzles/norinori/grade.ts`: ladder `region of two` (1), `domino completion` (1), `no touching` (2), `region pairs` (3), `assumption` (4), `deep assumption` (5).
- `src/puzzles/norinori/generate.ts`: plants non-touching dominoes, grows edge-connected regions around them, hill-climbs region boundaries until `countSolutions === 1`, keeps a candidate only when the grader solves it at exactly the target difficulty. Registered as `norinori` version 1 in `src/puzzles/generators.ts`.
- Tests written, sliced like star battle: `generate.test.ts`, `generate.property.ts`, `generate.property-{1,2,3}.test.ts`.

## Why blocked

- Over seeds `fixture-0` to `fixture-19` at difficulty 1, seeds 0, 2 and 7 hit the 4000-candidate cap and threw after about 125 to 135 s each. Successes took 10 to 60 s, up to 1848 candidates.
- `time-generators` over 50 seeds crashed on the same cap. Only d1 finished: median 245 ms, worst 1445 ms, yield 7.5%.
- Hand-picked seeds do better (d3 2 to 6 s, d4 about 50 ms, d5 1 to 14 s at sizes 7 to 8), but AC2 needs 100 seeds per difficulty with no failures.
- About 96% of repairs end non-unique after 60 steps. Most unique layouts grade 4, so tiers 2 and 3 accept rarely.
- Tried without effect: size ranges per difficulty, region-growth bias, repair step, move and rival counts, excluding planted cells from moves.

## Follow-up

- Build uniqueness in by construction (small forced-chain regions or extra constraints), or add a node budget to `engine.ts` `solutions()` (a performance fix the ticket allows).
- Decide the AC2 seed count and the candidate cap.
