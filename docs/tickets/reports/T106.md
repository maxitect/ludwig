## Design

- `railroad/generate.ts`: generator `railroad`, version 1, registered (generator and grader) in `src/puzzles/generators.ts`. Per candidate: a size from the difficulty (d1 4-5, d2 5-6, d3 6-8, d4 7-9, d5 8-10), two edge cells at least size-1 apart, a random self-avoiding walk between them of 30% to 70% of the cells (depth-first, node budget 4000), counts derived, every piece revealed, then pieces taken back in random order while `countSolutions === 1` and the grader still finishes at or below the target. For d5 the costliest technique is only tried on 8 pieces after the cheap pass. Kept when the grader's hardest technique equals the target.
- Bend threshold: a track with fewer bends than 40% of its cells (`MIN_BEND_SHARE`) is rejected.
- `railroad/grade.ts`: ladder, easiest first. 1 row and column counts, and "straight on and round the bend" (a cell with two known pieces closes the rest; a track cell with only two ways out opens both); 2 dead end (a cell with fewer than two ways out is off); 3 no loops, no early finish (a join that closes a loop, or links entry track to exit track while the counts want more track, is closed); 4 contradiction on a cell (a cell state that breaks a rule after level 2 propagation settles it the other way); 5 contradiction on a cell or piece (same with a piece of track as the hypothesis, propagating with level 3 rules). Crossings cannot arise in the edge model: a cell has at most two pieces of track.
- Performance fix to the engine (allowed by the ticket): the old depth-first `countSolutions` did not finish on 9x9 puzzles with few given pieces (minutes). `search` now decides one piece of track at a time after the grader's techniques (up to level 4) have drawn every conclusion, in both ways. It is exact (the 43 existing engine/verify/check tests pass unchanged) and takes milliseconds. The state and level 1 to 3 rules moved to `propagate.ts`, the side tables to `geometry.ts` (re-exported from `engine.ts`).
- A grader result of "solved" is a proof of uniqueness, since each technique is sound; `countSolutions` is still asserted separately.
- SPEC "Generated content" lists railroad.
