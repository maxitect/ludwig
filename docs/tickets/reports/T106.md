## Design

- `railroad/generate.ts`: generator `railroad`, version 1, registered (generator and grader) in `src/puzzles/generators.ts`. Per candidate: a size from the difficulty (d1 4-5, d2 5-6, d3 6-8, d4 7-9, d5 8-10), two edge cells at least size-1 apart, a random self-avoiding walk between them of 30% to 70% of the cells (depth-first, node budget 4000), counts derived, every piece revealed, then pieces taken back in random order while `countSolutions === 1` and the grader still finishes at or below the target. Kept when the grader's hardest technique equals the target.
- Bend threshold: a track with fewer bends than 40% of its cells (`MIN_BEND_SHARE`) is rejected.
- `railroad/grade.ts`: ladder, easiest first. 1 row and column counts, and "straight on and round the bend" (a cell with two known pieces closes the rest; a track cell with only two ways out opens both); 2 dead end (a cell with fewer than two ways out is off); 3 no loops, no early finish (a join that closes a loop, or links entry track to exit track while the counts want more track, is closed); 4 crossing parity (the track crosses a line between two rows or columns an odd number of times exactly when the entry and exit lie on opposite sides, so the last undecided piece across it follows) and cut off (a cell the entry can no longer reach is off); 5 bottleneck (a cell or piece of track that every way from the entry to some known track passes through is on). No technique makes a hypothesis and propagates it: the contradiction techniques of the first version were removed in review as trial and error (SPEC §4 authoring rule, research §5.2). Crossings cannot arise in the edge model: a cell has at most two pieces of track.
- Performance fix to the engine (allowed by the ticket): the old depth-first `countSolutions` did not finish on 9x9 puzzles with few given pieces (minutes). `search` now decides one piece of track at a time after the techniques up to level 4 have drawn every conclusion, in both ways. It is exact: the 43 existing engine/verify/check tests pass unchanged, and on 24,615 random layouts (3x3 to 7x7, 0 to 30 solutions, some with wrong counts or a wrong given) its counts (limit 30) and unique tracks equal the old search's (`.verification/T106/compare.ts`). With two different pieces given on one cell (which the `railroad_given_pieces` primary key rules out) it finds no track where the old search let the last one win. The state and level 1 to 3 rules moved to `propagate.ts`, the side tables to `geometry.ts` (re-exported from `engine.ts`).
- A grader result of "solved" is a proof of uniqueness, since each technique is sound; `countSolutions` is still asserted separately.
- SPEC "Generated content" lists railroad.
