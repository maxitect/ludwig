## Design

- `src/puzzles/circle9/generate.ts`: generator `circle9` version 1. A random solved sudoku supplies a transversal (one cell per row, column and box, distinct digits) that becomes the circles. Decoys with random digits are tried on the other cells in shuffled order, each kept only while `countSolutions === 1` and the grader, held to the target difficulty, still solves the grid, up to a quota of numbers (24, 30, 36, 42, 48 for difficulty 1 to 5, matching the hand-written files). A candidate is accepted when it grades at the target.
- `src/puzzles/circle9/grade.ts`: ladder over the 36 slots (digits, rows, columns, boxes): only cell (1); line in a line, a fish of one (2); starved line, a cell is out when circling it leaves a line with nothing to circle (3); fish of 2, 3 and 4 lines, for any two kinds of line (3, 4, 5). Registered in `generators.ts` for `puzzles:grade`.
- Deviation from the ticket wording: decoys are added to a planted grid rather than changed until unique; the first draft (fill the grid, clear decoys until unique, then reduce) collapsed to the nine circled numbers at every difficulty.
