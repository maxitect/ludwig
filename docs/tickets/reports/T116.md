## Notes

- `useTouchPhone()` added to the keyboard store (exported from the index). The cipher key strip is portalled into the solve slot on any touch phone, not only while the pad shows, so a hardware key or the device-keyboard setting never moves a focused slot between parents.
- `CipherKeyPanel`: slots take `inputmode="none"` while a pad is mounted, a tap (`onPointerDown`) calls `showPad`, and pad letters go to the focused slot, or the first unlocked slot if none is. Two rows of slots (`ceil(n/2)` columns) on a `paper-sheet`, since ink glyphs and borders vanish on the Ink desk. Pictogram symbols are drawn smaller on touch so a message fits above the strip. Clear moves into the pad's action row, or a row under the strip when the pad is off.
- `DigitGrid`: `digits` pad with 1..size, Notes and Clear notes in the action row, shown inline when the pad is off (same pattern as the crossword). Single aria-live status kept in the main area (`sr-only` on touch).
- Book cipher: `reference-bar` above the pad; with the pad on, the page section is capped at `--solver-height` (scrolls inside) and sits above the references. The intro paragraphs of the book and cipher solvers become `sr-only` while the pad is wanted, to give the area back.
- Added `data-testid="ciphertext"` and `reference-bar`. SPEC section 4.5 updated.
