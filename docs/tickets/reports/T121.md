## Deviations and follow-ups

- The shadow uses `var(--cast)`: blue-grey in Paper, paper at 45% in Ink (SPEC §6 token), so in Ink the second pass shadows the first and the cast fades toward its tip.
- `SideToMove` lives in `src/puzzles/reverse-chess/` (only reverse chess uses it). It and the uncapture tray put the glyph on a `bg-paper` tile, allowlisted in `raw-tokens.test.ts`; `pieces.tsx`, `last-move.tsx` and `unwind.tsx` left the allowlist, and `--piece-black-outline` is gone from `globals.css`.
- Rota draws name tokens, not `PieceGlyph`, so it is unchanged. The hub and landing title sequence use `PieceGlyph colour="white"` and pick up the ink outline and the haloed cross.
- SPEC §5.1 (side-to-move) and §6.5 (outlines, shadow, labels, off-board tiles, king cross) updated.
