## Changes beyond the ticket text

- Ink `--destructive` is now `ludwig-red` at 65% over paper with `--destructive-foreground` ink. T120's plain red was 3.2:1 on ink, which fails AA for the small error text T120 left to this ticket. The destructive button in Ink therefore has a lighter red fill with ink text.
- Paper `--ring` changed from grid-blue (1.8:1 on ink cells) to book-blue. SPEC §6.2, §6.4 (disabled button row) and §8.3 and `.claude/rules/design-system.md` updated.
- Chess cursor ring uses `var(--ring)` instead of a hard-coded grid-blue.
- Crossed-out items in logic grid and word search use `text-muted-foreground` (T120 follow-up; was `text-ink-soft`, about 2:1 in Ink).
- Gear crank "+" and "-" are lucide `Plus`/`Minus`; `size="icon"` svgs are `size-6` at stroke 2 (24px, 2px verified).
