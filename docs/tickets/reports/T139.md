## Deviations

- The cap's tooth list in `src/puzzles/gears/limits.ts` had to widen from `[8, 16]` to `[8, 12, 16]`, although the ticket puts "the cap itself" out of scope: without it `verifyStored` would reject any 12-tooth daily. The cap was measured on gear count (11 kept), and the layout guard confirms teeth do not change layout count. Taken as the recommended option.
- Easy keeps 8 and 16: with 12 teeth the crank period becomes 48 and 7 gears cannot be covered (every easy seed failed "No unique diagram" in a 10-seed probe; medium, hard and expert all succeeded). Easy dailies therefore draw only 8 and 16.
- 24 teeth was not used: 8,16,24 behaved the same as 12 but widens the cap further; 12 alone meets the three-size AC.
- "Bump the generator version": gear dailies have no generator version (`gear_daily` has only date and puzzle id), and `puzzles:verify` only re-solves them rather than regenerating. A new function `generateDailyDiagram` (with `dailyPresets`) is used by `generateDailies`; `generateDiagram`, curated presets and Fix the Diagram are unchanged, so nothing stored changes and nothing can regenerate or reject stored rows.
