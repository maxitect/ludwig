## Deviations and follow-ups

- The ticket's "within T119's cap" and AC1 can't both hold: the cap allowed two sizes and AC1 needs three. `gearCap.teeth` widened to `[8, 12, 16]`. With the old cap a new daily fails: `a gear has 12 teeth, only 8 and 16 are allowed`. The 11-gear limit is unchanged, and the AC2 frame times match `final-curtain`.
- Easy keeps 8 and 16. 30 seeds at the full 2000 attempts each (60,000 draws per row):

  ```
  easy 8,16        ok 30 fail 0
  easy 8,12        ok 0 fail 30
  easy 12,16       ok 0 fail 30
  easy 8,12,16     ok 0 fail 30
  easy 12          ok 0 fail 30
  8,12 and 8,12,16 at 30 degrees: ok 0 fail 30 each
  8,12 and 8,12,16 at 8 gears:    ok 0 fail 30 each
  easy 8,12,16 at 9 gears: ok 30 fail 0
  ```

  This is a search result, not a proof. The only working option found is 9 gears, which is medium's size, so easy would stop being easier.
- Because `shapeOf` sizes the search by the preset's lcm, every medium, hard and expert daily contains both 12 and 16 teeth; a diagram with a shorter period would repeat its win and be rejected. 8 teeth appear in 365 of the 1096 non-easy dailies swept.
- 24 teeth was not used: it widens the cap further and 12 alone meets AC1.
- "Bump the generator version": gears have no entry in `src/puzzles/generators.ts`, and `puzzles:verify` only re-solves `gear_daily` rows. `generateDiagram`, curated presets and Fix the Diagram are unchanged, so nothing stored changes.
- Production already holds dailies through 2027-11-05 and the ticket keeps them, so the first wider daily players see is 2027-11-06.
- SPEC §5.2.3 updated (cap teeth, daily teeth).
- `pnpm build` failed once on a transient Turbopack Google Fonts error; after removing `.next` it passed, and the gates passed.
