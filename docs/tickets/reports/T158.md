## Notes and deviations

- "Every entry has an unchecked letter" is enforced for Quick and Cryptic as asked. "No checked letter" cannot occur in a connected grid, so the unit test exercises it through the exported `gridStandardProblems`.
- Quick lattice: blocks sit on the (odd, odd) cells plus a symmetric set of extra blocks on rows and columns 0, 4, 6, 8, 12, chosen by an exhaustive search; 165 patterns met the rules, all have four entries of 10+ letters. Checked ratio is 32% to 33%, close to the 30% floor (the lattice is naturally about a third checked).
- The SCOWL list (`content/words.txt`) stops at 9 letters and has no commonness data, so fill came from SCOWL levels 10 and 20 (common words, via the `wordlist-english` package, scratch only) with a hand list of long words. Fills were curated by banning ugly entries and swapping (BEWARED, GAY) before clueing. Scratch tools stay in the gitignored `.verification/T158/`, not committed.
- Ratio caveat for Minis: all five sit at 61% to 65% checked (inside 60% to 75%).
- Quick shelf "difficulty" is 2 to 3; clues are definitions and short synonyms only.
