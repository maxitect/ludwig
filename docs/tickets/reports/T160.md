## What was built

- Migration `drizzle/20261107230526_desk_chart_views`: `user_daily_solves`, `user_type_stats`, `user_difficulty_stats`, as `pgView`s in `src/db/schema/progress.ts`, all reading `user_solves` (days via `at time zone 'Europe/London'` in SQL). None depends on another new view, so the alphabetical emit order is safe.
- `src/lib/data/desk-charts.ts` (`getDeskCharts`, called from `getDesk`'s `Promise.all`): reads the views plus per-week sums (`date_trunc('week', day)` over `user_daily_solves`), weekly-pair weeks (both `weekly_puzzles` solved), published-per-category and the last ten solve times per type. No solution data, no stored counters.
- `src/components/desk/`: `stat-tiles.tsx` (server, CSS count-up), `desk-charts.tsx` (client, `next/dynamic` `ssr:false`), `charts-view.tsx` (lazy chunk root), `activity-chart.tsx`, `weekly-line.tsx`, `category-bars.tsx`, `type-tiles.tsx`, `speed-chart.tsx`, `chart-parts.tsx` (intro transitions, tooltip, hidden table), `chart-model.ts`.
- The Desk's last shelf now renders stat tiles, then the charts (or the Walker and "Solve a few more and the charts fill in" under 3 solves), then the Casebook link. The Casebook is unchanged.
- `.claude/rules/design-system.md` allows D3 transitions on the Desk charts and records the chart conventions.
- D3 modules pinned exact: `d3-array` 3.2.4, `d3-scale` 4.0.2, `d3-selection` 3.0.0, `d3-shape` 3.2.0, `d3-transition` 3.0.1, plus `@types/*` (dev).
- Design choices: no categorical palette exists (ink for data, `ludwig-red` for the highlighted series or selection, `grid-blue` grid lines, `ink:stroke-muted` for the same lines on Ink because `grid-blue` vanishes on the dark surface), so the palette validator had nothing to validate. Every red mark also differs by shape or label (outline, square marker, "latest" bar called out in the caption), and each chart has a legend or caption.

- Something moves on arrival: the numbers count up, bars grow, squares pop in by column, the line draws, spark bars rise (about 500 ms, staggered). Yes.
