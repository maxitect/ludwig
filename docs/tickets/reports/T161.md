## Migration

`drizzle/20261111084032_user_type_activity_view`: one `CREATE VIEW user_type_activity` (pgView in `src/db/schema/progress.ts`, T023/T160 pattern). No table, column or stored score, so "store nothing" holds; the migration is needed only because the ticket asks for a view and a view is a migration artefact. It is additive and safe against the previous deploy. It reads `attempts` directly (not `user_solves`, which has no unfinished attempts) and uses `Europe/London` days from `started_at`, `last_saved_at` and `completed_at`.

## What was built

- `src/lib/recommend.ts`: pure `recommend()`, with `WEIGHTS` and `THRESHOLDS` constants at the top. Signals: favourite, solves played (capped), fast against other types at the same difficulty, step up in difficulty (difficulty 2 for an untried type), discovery in the most-played category, recent (14 days), comeback rate ((active days - 1) / days since first day), returns after a solve; negatives for unfinished count, abandonment ratio, stale unfinished (untouched over 7 days), hints above 1.5x the category average, median over 2x other types, tried once over 30 days ago, dormant over 60 days unless favourite. Top positive signal supplies the reason. Resets are noted in the docstring as unrecordable.
- Choices beyond the ticket: at most 2 puzzles per type so one favourite cannot fill the shelf; "stale" uses `last_saved_at` rather than `started_at` so a long attempt still being worked on is not counted as bounced.
- `src/lib/data/recommendations.ts` (`getRecommendations`): cached candidate read (`cacheTag("puzzles")`, excludes daily, gear daily and weekly puzzles in SQL), cached popular-types read for cold start (all users, from `user_solves`, falling back to catalogue order for unsolved types), and per-user reads of the activity view, `user_type_stats`, per-type-and-difficulty medians, favourites and solved ids. Wired into `getDesk`.
- Desk shelf `RecommendedShelf` in `desk.tsx`, between Today/This week and the stats: `PuzzleThumbnail`, title, type, difficulty badge and reason. Desk order: Continue, Favourites, Today, This week, Recommended, Charts. Cold start adds a "Popular starts" line and "Popular start" reasons.
- `e2e/recommendations.spec.ts` and `completeAttemptFor` helper in `e2e/helpers/db.ts`.

## Deviations and notes

- The scorer tests sit in `src/lib/`, which the Vitest config runs in the `db` project (pure, but needs no special setup).
- The user's solves-per-type median ratio compares at the difficulty the player has played that type most; with no other type at that difficulty there is no ratio and neither the fast nor the slow signal applies.
- A small `getViewSelectedFields` spread is used to join the view to `puzzle_types` for `category_key`.
- Self-assessed visuals: shelf matches the Favourites/Today card styling in Paper; no overflow at 390 and 1280.
