## Deviations and follow-ups

- **Dependencies:** `three` 0.186.1, `@react-three/fiber` 9.8.2, `@types/three` 0.186.0, exact. No drei. The scene chunk is about 249 KB gzipped (three and r3f are about 240 KB of it, `SVGLoader` about 9 KB), loaded only by the Desk. r3f 9.8.2 logs a `THREE.Clock` deprecation warning; there is nothing to do on our side.
- **Pre-existing gap found:** the signed-in Desk streams inside `Suspense`, so without JavaScript the whole Desk stays in a hidden container. To satisfy AC4 the Desk page's fallback now has a `<noscript>` with the empty-desk SVG. The shelves are still invisible without JS; fixing that is a separate change.
- `scripts/dev/lighthouse.ts` gained an optional `DESK_COOKIE` env var that adds the signed-in Desk to its page list (skipped when unset, so CI is unchanged). I did not add a cookie bootstrap for CI.
- The pawn path moved into `src/puzzles/_shared/chess-board/piece-paths.ts` so the 3D scene extrudes the same geometry as the SVG pieces; `pieces.tsx` imports it.
- Rules for 3D were added to `.claude/rules/design-system.md` ("Three.js scenes").
- New tests: `src/components/desk/desk-scene/model.test.ts`, `e2e/desk-scene.spec.ts`.
- The orbit uses azimuth steps of 4 degrees up to 16; mouse hover maps to the same range, touch drags.
- Screenshots and clips are in `.verification/T162/` (git-ignored). No video clip; `desk-orbit-left/right-390.png` show the interaction.
