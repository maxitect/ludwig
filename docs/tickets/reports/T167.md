## Evidence

Design: four `.seq-room` groups (`--room` 0..3) at `translateX(room * 70%)` inside one `preserve-3d` world, so each room's right wall is the next room's left wall. One continuous floor spans all rooms. The camera is two animations on the world, `transform: translateX(-k * 70%)` (ease-in-out across each pan) and `rotate: y <yaw>` (ease-in-out to a lead of 4 deg, 6 deg on desktop, toward the next room at mid-pan and back to calm), which compose to the same `rotateY(yaw) translateX(...)` matrix but ease independently. Dwell windows unchanged (0.06-0.26, 0.41-0.58, 0.72-0.86), pans 0.26-0.41, 0.58-0.72, 0.86-0.96. On the animated path each divider is a 700px partition (ending 200px behind the stage plane, with an ink edge), so the next room shows past its end; the Grid's left wall and the finale's right wall stay 1200px. Room wall opacity swaps, `.seq-wall-grid/board/mirror` animations and the peak/forward-translate camera were removed. Captions fade around the pans.

## Deviations and follow-ups

- The finale space is a fourth staged room whose back wall carries the faint finale digits; the `.seq-finale` section below the sticky stage is unchanged.
- Divider and left wall are two one-sided planes on the same plane (backface culling), not a single translucent wall.
- In reduced motion the far rooms are `display: none` and the walls keep their full 1200px depth, so the static composition is the Grid room only, closed as before.
- Old AC1 swap-window and T156 yaw-through-crossing e2e tests were replaced by the new ones; SPEC §6.3 "Title-sequence walls" updated. Added `scripts/dev/landing-strip.mts` (frame-strip recorder).
- Mobile pans cover the narrow viewport quickly (the rooms are 70% of a 390px stage) but remain continuous.
