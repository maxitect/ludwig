## Evidence

Design: four `.seq-room` groups (`--room` 0..3) at `translateX(room * 70%)` inside one `preserve-3d` world, so each room's right wall is the next room's left wall. One continuous floor spans all rooms. The camera is the world transform `rotateY(yaw) translateX(-k * 70%)`: dwell windows unchanged (0.06-0.26, 0.41-0.58, 0.72-0.86), pans 0.26-0.41, 0.58-0.72, 0.86-0.96, yaw dipping 8 deg (14 deg desktop) toward the next room mid-pan and back to calm. The divider is a partition ending before the camera plane, so the camera passes its plane in the open and the next room shows beside it. Room wall opacity swaps, `.seq-wall-grid/board/mirror` animations and the peak/forward-translate camera were removed. Captions fade around the pans.

## Deviations and follow-ups

- Divider and left wall are two one-sided planes on the same plane (backface culling), not a single translucent wall.
- In reduced motion the far rooms are `display: none`, so the static composition is the Grid room only. At the start pose the divider is now visible at the right of the Grid room.
- Old AC1 swap-window and T156 yaw-through-crossing e2e tests were replaced by the new ones; SPEC §6.3 "Title-sequence walls" updated. Added `scripts/dev/landing-strip.mts` (frame-strip recorder).
