## Evidence

### Design

- `src/components/puzzle/solve-chrome.tsx`: from `lg` with a fine pointer, the `<main>` gets `data-solve-layout` (`panel` or `board-only`, from `src/config/solve-layout.ts`). Two CSS variants in `globals.css`, `desk:` (compact title bar, 12px page padding) and `panel:` (the grid), match `(min-width: 64rem) and not touch`. The section is a grid: title bar over both columns, the board column (`--solver-height: 100dvh - 164px`, floor 24rem), and the panel column with rows head, body (1fr, scrolls), foot, Check and Reset, status, solved footer. The solved footer replaces Check and Reset and is capped at the panel's height so the stamp stays whole.
- `src/puzzles/_shared/solve-layout.tsx`: `SolveLayout` (root, `display: contents` on desktop), `BoardCell` (sets `--board-h`, `--board-w`, `--cell-cap` and the size container) and `PanelCell` (`head`, `body`, `foot`). Below `lg` and under `touch` they are `display: contents`, so the stacked layout is each solver's own. `capWidth` and `heightWidth` lift the cell caps to 4.5rem and bound boards by the board height.
- Boards bounded by height: `CellGrid`, `HighlightPathGrid` (through `snappedCell`), `RegionMarkGrid`, `TrainBoard`, the chess board and its placeholder (one shared class, `chess-board/board-size.ts`), `RotaBoard`, the wheel, the street scene, nonogram, reflections, railroad, sightlines, cctv-maze, the book page and the logic grid's `MarkGrid` (cell and label band derived from `--board-h`).
- Panel scrolling: unwind chain (latest ply scrolled into view with `scrollWithin`, which moves only the panel), crossword (`ClueTabs split`: both lists at once on desktop, tabs elsewhere), logic grid, knights and knaves, rota, book cipher references. `scrollWithin` is also used to bring the logic grid's wrong-pair list into view.

## Deviations and follow-ups

- **SPEC §4.5** is rewritten (the "Wide solve layout" bullet). Panel column is 24rem (384px, the audit's minimum for the footer) with a 24px gap rather than the audit's 416px and 32px, because the book cipher's 56-character line has to fit at 1024px; the book cipher alone has an 18rem panel (`narrowPanelSolveTypes`).
- **Word ladder** keeps its start and end words in the rung list, so the list stays one ordered list; on desktop they sit in a second column beside the rungs (changed in review, so the ladder fits at 1280x650). The audit put them in the panel, which would split the list. Only the prompt is in the panel.
- **Reflections**: the AC3 test measures the sheet (the grid plus its one-cell camera margin), 592px on its shorter side at 1440x800. **Nonogram** and **kakuro** are within a few pixels of the budget because their clue bands are estimated.
- **DOM order**: where a solver's heading comes before its board (chess, cipher and clue prompts), the heading stays first in the DOM and sits in the panel's head area; the board is not first. Reordering would have changed the phone layout.
- **Knights and knaves** has no framed statement (the check returns a count), so AC7 uses `manifest`; its wrong-pair list is scrolled into the panel.
- **`anagram` and `odd-one-out`** stay `board-only` (open: in view at both sizes). Solved, the footer sits below the fold at 1280x650, as the audit documented; not taken to the chrome-only rail.
- **Updated tests** (changed behaviour, not weakened): `cell-grid.test.tsx` (the cap is now `calc(cols * max(cellRem, var(--cell-cap)))`), `sightlines-brief.spec.ts` (brief is beside the grid on desktop, above it on touch), `spot-difference-desktop.spec.ts` (crossword and sudoku now use the wide main on desktop by design; the reading-column check runs on `gears`).
- **`e2e/desktop-layout.spec.ts`** was written by a forked helper and run by me; Reverse Chess `unwind` solved overflowed by 10px until the panel foot (ply choices, notation) was hidden once solved.
- Run in review: the preview checks and Lighthouse (AC14), the solved half of the audit (AC1), and the signed-in a11y, `iphone` and `tablet` specs (WebKit installed).

- **24rem panel, 24px gap, 18rem for the book cipher.** 24rem is the audit's own minimum for the solved footer (section 3), and the footer, stamp and time fit at every type and both sizes (audit run above). The narrower gap is why `detective-scene` now reaches 536px.
- **Unwind hides `PlyChoices` and `NotationToggle` once solved.** They are inside the `inert` wrapper then, and hiding them is what lets the stamp fit at 1280x650.
- **Edited tests.** `cell-grid.test.tsx` asserts the new cap expression exactly; `sightlines-brief.spec.ts` still asserts position (beside the grid on desktop, above it under touch); `spot-difference-desktop.spec.ts` keeps the reading-column check on `gears`, the one type still in it. None was loosened.
- **AC7 on `manifest` only.** Knights and knaves returns a count, so there is no statement to frame; its status line is in the panel.
- **`SolveSlot`** is not used by `solve-layout.tsx` or the panel; the cells render inside `[data-testid=solver]`, which carries `inert`; the five journeys assert focus in the solved footer.
- **`gears` and `spot-difference`:** element boxes identical to production at 1440x800, 1280x650 and 390x844.
