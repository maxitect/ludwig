## Evidence

What was built:

- **Stage under the header.** `.seq-stage` is `position: sticky; top: var(--seq-header); height: calc(100dvh - var(--seq-header))`. `--seq-header` has a CSS estimate for first paint (4.13rem, 4.375rem on `pointer: coarse`) and `ScrollFallback` (always mounted) sets the measured header height through a `ResizeObserver`. Scroll progress is unchanged (0 with the track top at the viewport top, 1 at the track's end). The track is 380dvh (was 400dvh, one pan fewer).
- **One room per shot.** Dividers stay 1200px so their near ends are off screen at a dwell. What had to change: the yaw. The fixed 900px perspective means a wide yaw puts a wall's near end into frame on narrower stages (calculated in `.verification/T171/calc.py`, confirmed by the tests): calm/open angles are now 4deg/0 below 48rem, 8deg/-8deg to 64rem, 12deg/-12deg to 80rem and 16deg/-16deg above (they were 12/16deg at 768 and 1024, which showed the cut end there).
- **End on the Mirror.** Fourth room, third divider side, finale wall and third pan removed; pans at 0.26-0.41 and 0.58-0.72, the Mirror holds from 0.72 to the end (caption 3 now holds too). `.seq-finale` renamed `.seq-closing`.
- **Two Chromium rendering faults found and fixed** (neither shows in a rectangle test):
  1. A floor plane touching the walls' bottom edge made Chromium's 3D sorting drop the far part of a wall mid-pan (a black strip beside the back wall at about 0.30). First fixed with a 1px gap under the walls; the review replaced that with one floor plane in its own 3D context, painted before the walls (see Review).
  2. A one pixel crack where a back wall met a divider showed the next room's wall (301 px at 390 wide). Back walls are inset 2px from the shared plane.
- **Measuring helper** `ownership()` in `e2e/landing-walls.spec.ts`: injects a style painting each room's planes flat in one colour channel, screenshots the stage, counts the pixels each room covers, and reads the left and right edge pixel columns. Reworked in review (see Review).

Reading of the strips (AC13), all four sizes, both themes, same at each:

- First frame (0): the Grid only, hero card wholly under the header, nothing behind the header; side walls reach the frame edges.
- Grid dwell (0.16): the Grid back wall, its left wall and the divider on the right fill the frame; the divider runs out of the frame, no gap, no Board.
- Pan 1 (0.34): the camera slides along the divider; the Grid's back wall slides left, the divider edge sweeps across, and a slice of the Board's wall appears at the right edge beyond the divider's near end. Only the Grid and the Board.
- Board dwell (0.50): the Board's wall, queens-side wall on the left, divider on the right; no Grid, no Mirror.
- Pan 2 (0.65): same, Board leaving, Mirror entering.
- Mirror dwell and end (0.86, 1.0): the Mirror with its five red digits written; the right wall closes the scene, nothing beyond; the closing section follows.
- No frame shows a dwell with another room or a cut wall end, a third room, a room behind the header or a faded fourth room. At 390 wide the hero card covers most of the first frame; the room is the same.
