## Changes

- `src/lib/data/*`: `cacheLife("minutes")` becomes `"hours"` (catalogue, gears, previews, weekly, reverse-chess, recommendations candidates, puzzles).
- Nav, mobile nav, footer About, guest-menu Sign in, logo, `/puzzles` type cards and type-page puzzle links: `prefetch={false}`. Prefetch is not a stated requirement anywhere in SPEC section 6.
- `saveTheme`, `saveSettings`: `refresh()` replaces `revalidatePath("/", "layout")`. Theme applies client side (`applyTheme`, cookie) so nothing shared depended on it.
- SPEC section 7.3 (cache life, worst-case delay, no layout purge) and 7.7 (prefetch rule).
- Midnight delay: the shelf, `/puzzles` and `/this-week` can lag up to about one hour after London midnight, and a stale entry is served once while regenerating. Stated in SPEC. No midnight cron added: Hobby crons fire at an unpredictable point in their hour, so they would not beat the cache life.
