- No `src/`, `content/` or `e2e/` file changed. `docs/research/desktop-solve-panel-audit.md` is new, `docs/tickets/done/T175-desktop-solve-panel-remediation.md` is updated, `scripts/dev/audit-desktop-layout.mts` gains the `--panel`, `--widths` and `--browsers` modes.
- **Estimates in the audit** are marked *est.*: the rota's Unswaps list and its accusation in two columns (196px), the grown pictogram ciphertext, and the gears crank stepper and Accuse in one row. T175 measures them when it builds them. The others (the rota's pinned list in two columns, the cipher key in the left sheet, the book cipher's prompt and list, the knights and knaves cards, the gears brief and swap panel at 384px) were measured in review by restyling the live page and are marked *probed* (see Review). The solved footers of `rota`, `nonogram` and `odd-one-out` use stand-in text of the real length, because a completion row has no epilogue.
- **Daily gears:** a local daily (`daily-2026-11-24`) was generated with `pnpm puzzles:top-up-dailies` in the worktree's own database; production dailies are capped at the same 11 gears (`limits.ts`), so the largest curated diagram is the largest daily.
- **The audit changes two numbers T170 recorded:** boards lose 12px of height to the 24px footer gap (624 and 474px), and the classic scrollbars give a 793px cell (T170 recorded 808px, measured with overlay bars). Both are stated in the audit's section 5 and section 8.
- **Classic scrollbars depend on the machine.** Headless Chromium without `--hide-scrollbars` follows macOS: classic 15px bars with a mouse attached or "Show scroll bars" set to "Always", overlay bars otherwise. The audit's run was classic; the review re-run was overlay (0px bars, an 808px cell, lists one wrap shorter). The script now prints the mode.
- WebKit on macOS cannot show classic scrollbars in headless mode, so its bar width is 0; geometry (gap, clipped rows) matches Chromium.
- The session was interrupted twice by the harness while the long run was in flight; the data files were produced by one uninterrupted final run (main, widths and browsers all exited 0). The `--widths` mode was fixed once (it measured against a hidden Check button and used keys that were deduplicated into the representatives) and re-run on its own, which is why its output is newer than `panel.json`.
