- No `src/`, `content/` or `e2e/` file changed. `docs/research/desktop-solve-panel-audit.md` is new, `docs/tickets/T175-desktop-solve-panel-remediation.md` is updated, `scripts/dev/audit-desktop-layout.mts` gains the `--panel`, `--widths` and `--browsers` modes.
- **Estimates in the audit** are marked *est.*: the rota's pinned list in two columns (150px), the rota's accusation in two columns (196px), the cipher key in 13 columns and the book cipher's 24px prompt saving. T175 measures them when it builds them. The solved footers of `rota`, `nonogram` and `odd-one-out` use stand-in text of the real length, because a completion row has no epilogue.
- **Daily gears:** a local daily (`daily-2026-11-24`) was generated with `pnpm puzzles:top-up-dailies` in the worktree's own database; production dailies are capped at the same 11 gears (`limits.ts`), so the largest curated diagram is the largest daily.
- **The audit changes two numbers T170 recorded:** boards lose 12px of height to the 24px footer gap (624 and 474px), and the classic scrollbars give a 793px cell (T170 recorded 808px, measured with overlay bars). Both are stated in the audit's section 5 and section 8.
- WebKit on macOS cannot show classic scrollbars in headless mode, so its bar width is 0; geometry (gap, clipped rows) matches Chromium.
- The session was interrupted twice by the harness while the long run was in flight; the data files were produced by one uninterrupted final run (main, widths and browsers all exited 0). The `--widths` mode was fixed once (it measured against a hidden Check button and used keys that were deduplicated into the representatives) and re-run on its own, which is why its output is newer than `panel.json`.
