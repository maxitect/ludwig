## Evidence

### Design

- `src/puzzles/_shared/panel-section.tsx`: `PanelSection`, a native `<details>` with a 32px summary and a square caret (`panel-caret`), an optional `name` (exclusive group), controlled `open`/`onOpenChange`, `keepOpen`, `scroll` and `rows`. Below `lg` and under `touch` the summary is hidden and `::details-content` draws the content (`panel-section-flat`), and the sections are opened after hydration so the accessibility tree holds the content (nothing moves, it was already drawn). `disclosure` keeps a plain closed `<details>` there (the rota's How to play).
- `src/puzzles/_shared/use-whole-rows.ts`: cuts a scroll area at its last whole row (a `ResizeObserver` and a `MutationObserver`, desktop only, never runs under jsdom).
- `src/app/globals.css`: `@utility panel-scroll` (10px square bar, paper-shade track, ink thumb, `scrollbar-width: thin` only without `::-webkit-scrollbar`, 12px right and bottom padding, 2px hard rule), `panel-section`, `panel-section-flat`, `panel-caret`.
- Spacing is in `solve-chrome.tsx` and `solve-layout.tsx` only: Check row `pt-4`, section `pb-3` (`desk:`), `--solver-height` `100dvh - 176px`; `PanelCell` lost its `pb-3`, `px-1` and `overflow-y-auto`. `PanelCell` also hides its body and foot once solved (`keepSolved`/`hideSolved` opt out), which is the solved rule.
- Crossword: `ClueTabs split` renders two exclusive `PanelSection`s (`name="crossword-clues"`) driven by `direction`; `keepOpen` keeps one list open always; `scrollWithin` keeps the active clue in view.
- Classic gears: `gears` moved to `panelSolveTypes`; diagram in the board cell (`min(100%, --board-h)`: 576px at 1440x800, 474px at 1280x650), the state table in a second board cell that replaces it (the diagram stays mounted), the brief and the swap panel an exclusive pair, crank and Accuse share a row.
