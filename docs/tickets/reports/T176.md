## Budget (read 2026-11-27)

- Web Analytics, Hobby: 50,000 events/month, reporting window 1 month, no custom events or UTM. Over the limit: 3-day grace, then collection stops until the cycle resets (no charge; the page also says "Wait 7 days" in one place). https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/limits-and-pricing
- Speed Insights, free tier: 10,000 events over the last 30 days (shared across the team), Real Experience Score only, 24h and 7d ranges, no charge. Over the limit: collection pauses for at least 14 days, and stays paused while the last 30 days are above half the allowance. https://vercel.com/docs/speed-insights/limits-and-pricing
